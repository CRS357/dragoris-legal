Privacy Policy

This policy describes information processed by Dragoris when you interact with the Bot. Discord also processes information under its own privacy policy. The Bot is operated by an individual developer, not a separate company.

1. Information the Bot processes

  • Discord and command information: Discord user IDs and usernames, command names, command options or attributes, timestamps, and whether an interaction occurred in a direct message or server. Server names and IDs may also be recorded for server interactions. The Bot records slash-command interaction details in its local command log.
  • Access-control information: User IDs on allowlists or blocklists and any associated module permissions.
  • AI conversations: If you message the AI feature in a direct message, or mention it in an enabled channel, the message content and display name may be added to conversation history. The Bot stores one shared direct-message history and one shared public-channel history in local files; histories are not separated by individual user or server and may be backed up. The content needed to generate a reply is sent to an AI model running on a system controlled by the operator.
  • Event-planning submissions: If you participate in an event poll, your Discord user ID and the time or availability information you submit may be saved locally so the poll can be updated.
  • Advertisement data: If you use advertisement features, the advertisement title, description, links, image URL, fields, and associated advertiser Discord ID may be saved locally.
  • Media and profile features: A feature may process information needed to fulfill your request, such as a Discord user ID for a profile lookup, an avatar image for an image effect, or an uploaded file. A webcam command, if enabled and authorized, captures one image from the Bot host’s camera and sends it through Discord; the temporary image file is removed from the host after the attempt.
  • Spotify integration: Spotify features connect to the operator’s configured Spotify account. Spotify OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are kept in a local cache on the Bot host; requested playback information or controls are exchanged with Spotify and may be shown in Discord. Spotify handles that processing under its own policies.
  • Operational diagnostics: Error and runtime output may be sent to the operator through Discord direct messages or a configured channel to maintain and troubleshoot the Bot.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to provide requested features, maintain conversation context, manage access, update event polls or advertisements, carry out commands, secure and troubleshoot the Bot, and comply with legal obligations. The operator does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

3. Storage and retention

Bot records are stored in local files and a local database on the operator-controlled Bot host. Some records, including command logs and AI history backups, do not currently have an automatic deletion schedule and may remain until the operator removes them. The Bot’s AI history-clearing feature moves history into a backup rather than immediately erasing that backup. Retention of messages and other information on Discord is controlled separately by Discord and the relevant server.

Information is shared only as needed to operate a feature: with Discord when the Bot reads or sends Discord content, with the operator-controlled host running the AI model, with Spotify for the Spotify integration, and with image or media sources required by a requested feature.
